**State (2026-09-12):** the game is a phone web app in `web/` (JavaScript engine + one-file UI, PWA, GitHub Pages deploy in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`). **Comprehensive stats (2026-09-12, PR #10):** finished seasons are kept whole in `state.history` (never pruned), stats are rebuilt from the scorecards on load, saves are packed (~80 KB a season), the Fixtures screen has a season picker, the Table screen lists past seasons, and a player page has season-by-season rows plus an innings-by-innings match log. Save v3 with a v2 fixture. Age and the peak/decline curve already exist (hidden); see the backlog for the visible version.

## Open design questions

Surfaced 2026-06-02 while pressure-testing the Player Creation plan. Both are **auto-sim design decisions (Theme 7)** — non-blocking for the Player Creation build, but must be settled before/during the sim build. Both sit downstream of ADR 0004 (only-Player-statted; the 21 other players are derived from `teamStrength × role × seed`, not individually authored).

1. **Player participation model** — how much the Player personally bats (batting position → balls faced) and bowls (overs) each Match. Candidates: *fixed participation* (build changes only how **well**, not how **much**; bowler-shaped Players still bat, and bat badly emergently) vs *attribute-driven participation* (a bowler-shaped build bats lower / bowls more automatically). Either keeps the classifier label flavour-only per spec §3.5. **Simplest-first leaning: fixed.**
2. **Bowler-rotation policy** — how an innings' ~20 bowling overs get assigned across bowler-role profiles. *Player side* surfaces as agency via the **Bowling Change** Key Moment; *opponent side* needs an AI rotation policy (currently un-spec'd). Note the scope wall: opponents are regenerated per-Match with no persistent identity or cross-Match form, so "their star bowler is out of form" is **texture-via-commentary**, not simulation — the cheap third door that avoids rebuilding the deferred management sim.
3. **Individual player-stat realism — the player-as-bowler takes a disproportionate share of wickets (logged 2026-06-15, from interactive-match playtest).** In a sampled match (seed-20260615) the Player bowled 3 overs and took **6/18 — i.e. ALL six opponent wickets fell in his 18 balls**, the other 17 overs taking none. Root suspicion: the player's 3-over spell bowls at his own attack/control (concentrated), and the base DRS claim-review (ungated on the bowling side) can convert dots to wickets in his overs. **This is a NEW concern, orthogonal to the balance work that is "done":** the harness tuned **team win-rates + build-equality + pay**, never *individual* stat realism — so player batting/bowling figures can read unrealistically even though the match outcome is fair. Now that the match screen surfaces these figures (PR #69), they're visible to the player and worth their own calibration rung (measure the player-over wicket share vs a realistic ~per-bowler distribution; likely a participation/conservation tweak, not a ball-math change). **Not blocking presentation.**
