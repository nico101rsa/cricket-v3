Both lines rise toward the top-right: a stronger team qualifies more often and finishes higher. The S-shaped step in the middle is real — a hard top-4 cut on 8 teams is naturally sigmoid, so a small ★ gain near the median flips a lot of Seasons across the qualification line at once.
