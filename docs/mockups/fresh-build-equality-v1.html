Fresh-build-equality — "earn your position"

Win % across the batting↔bowling build axis at even ★3 vs ★3 (N=2000/build, build_spectrum_sweep). A balanced game should be a flat line at ~50% — your build should change how you win, not whether you win. Batting-position promotion is now gated by absolute batting competence (pos_ref_batting=70): a fresh weak batsman bats lower and climbs the order as he levels up.

before (share-only position)   after (competence-gated)

The fix. Before, at the fresh end every build is absolutely weak, so a batting-lean build got promoted to #3 and squandered high-leverage balls — dragging the team to 174 and losing (43%), while a bowling-lean build hid its weak bat at #7 and let the topped-up top order score 178+ (52%). Win % humped from 43 → 62 (spread 19 pts). After, weak players bat where their competence fits, so the curve flattens to ~51–53 (spread 2 pts). The mid/maxed game (SUM=125) was already flat and stays flat — the change only touches weak builds.
