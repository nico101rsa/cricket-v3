Three art styles for player identity

Same two players in each column — a batter (your captain, veteran, gold helmet) and a bowler (rookie). Shown at three sizes: large (squad/profile), medium (Reigns key-moment card), tiny (scorebar). Identity uses our own iconography — no IPL trade-dress.

What you're looking at

  • Same two players across all three styles: R. SHARMA (your captain — veteran, gold helmet, top-order bat) and A. KUMAR (rookie spinner, blue cap, fresh kit)
  • Achievement layer (IP-safe): gold helmet = your captain, gold frame on portrait = elite, blue trim = rookie. No IPL-style cap colours.
  • Wear layer: dirty/scuffed helmet on the captain (veteran of many seasons), clean kit on the rookie
  • Identity layer: beard on the captain, clean face on the rookie. Hair, build, skin would all vary per-player procedurally — seeded by player ID, never changes.

IP-safe achievement language proposed

  • Gold helmet/border — your team captain
  • ★ stars (1–4) — player tier, like FUT rating without the orange/purple cap pairing
  • CAPT / STAR / RKE / VET badges — text on portrait frame, not colour-coded league awards
  • Dirty equipment / scuffed helmet — emergent veteran signal as you suggested
  • Jersey colour — team identity (you pick palette per franchise)
  • No orange caps, no purple caps, no IPL-derived trade dress anywhere
