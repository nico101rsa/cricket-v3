Career loop v1 — naive-policy eyeball

Source: tools/career_preview.gd (spec 2026-06-12-career-loop-design.md DC15/§6). Policy: play the lowest unbeaten unlocked tour at the current Level, then farm Premier for the Level win; move up one Level only after winning the current one (a policy choice, not a rule — under the v2 sheet the next Level unlocks as soon as Tour 4 "Day Mixed" is beaten, and Province Premier itself has no gate per the 2026-06-12 ruling); spend ₸ on round-robin +1 attribute upgrades (cap 60/attribute, attr_cost_base re-pegged 11→13 this rung — prize income would otherwise max the build ~5 Seasons early); textbook batting/bowling plans; no jokers, no Shop (Season-scoped systems are not in the headless loop — see spec §4). Optimal play is E4's job; this page only shows the loop is traversable and the numbers move in the right directions.
