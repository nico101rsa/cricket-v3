Career-line balancing — the readiness gate (post-E4)

The E4 search found the 9 career lines (3 ways to climb × 3 ways to spend) wildly unequal: rush beat the game ~2× faster than anything else and two lines never finished at all. This rung raised the League-readiness gate — you now must clear 7 of 8 tours (everything but the Premier) before you can climb to the next League — and fixed the farm climb so it never softlocks. What changed, and the honest limit

Fixed (the real E4 bug): rush went from a 93% / 15.8h no-brainer to ~85% / 22–26h — line-ball with farm. No line now beats the game ~2× faster than another (time spread 2.25×+∞ → 1.57×), and all 9 lines complete (the two 0% farm softlocks are gone).
The wall we hit: completion is driven by how many attempts you get at the Province Premier final (1st of 8 — luck-heavy), not by card strength — a fresh-card rush (80%) out-finishes a maxed-card trophy (57%) because it reaches the final far more often. To give trophy equal completion the final would have to reward card strength — but that locks out joker_only (it never trains; jokers reset every season). So strict 9-way equality is impossible without flattening genuine roguelite trade-offs.
Ruling (2026-06-15): ship the gate; keep the residual spread as healthy texture — joker_only = fast/risky "hard mode", attr_only = slow/reliable, trophy = the long way round.
