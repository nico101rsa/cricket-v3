The Kit Room opens: three spending styles, 100 careers each

Every career: a fresh creation build (Power 35 / Composure 30 / Attack 30 / Control 30 on the /100 card scale), naive tour & offer choices (lowest unbeaten tour; move up once the League is won), textbook opponents plus each cell's difficulty-ladder brain, career cap 120 Seasons, seeds 9000–9099. The only difference between the three groups is what they do at the Shop: train only (+1 attribute per visit, never buys a joker), jokers only (buys the best joker it can afford every visit, never trains), balanced (sets aside the next training fee, buys a joker with what is left). All three take the free starter joker each Season — that is part of the game's rules, not a choice. Source: tools/career_preview.gd, one run per CAREER_POLICY arm, 2026-06-12.

Did the career get finished? (win Province Premier within 120 Seasons)

The pre-Shop baseline (same bot, no Shop at all, PR #50) finished 79 of 100 with a median of 62 Seasons. Training through the Shop's visit cadence plus the free starter joker beats that baseline on its own; balancing jokers and training on top of it finishes nearly every career.
