How each Joker shifts the match (7c-C slice)

How to read this: we played the same even match (your team ★3 vs an opponent ★3, a balanced 5/5/5/5 Player) 2000 times per arm — baseline (no jokers), then each of the 5 slice Jokers on its own, then the three batting Jokers stacked. Every arm uses the same intent plan (aggressive powerplay+death, defensive middle) so the only thing changing is the Joker. The X-axis is how many runs YOU personally scored (the runs curves barely move — most of these Jokers act on wicket-chance or on the bowling side). The number that matters is the win-rate column on the right.

The runs curves sit almost on top of each other — these Jokers mostly bend wicket chance or suppress the opposition's runs, neither of which moves your own score much. Win-rate tells the story: Powerplay Punch (runs +10% while aggressive) is the strongest single Common at ~+5.5% win-rate over baseline; the two bowling run-suppressors (Squeeze the Middle, Death-Over Stranglehold) add ~+3% each; the narrow-window Block the Shine / Dead Bat add under +1%. The batting stack (all 3 batting Jokers) reaches ~+7.4% — roughly the sum of its parts, so multiplicative stacking isn't running away yet. First OP/weak data for the balance harness.
