Why "just go aggro" used to win — and why it doesn't anymore

Per-match win-rate for the weakest team (Club slot-0, 1.5★, reference 35/30/30/30 build) vs the club field, through the real MatchSession. The matchup is held fixed — only the opponent's brain changes — so each bar isolates how much the AI's tactics contest you. N = 400 seasons × 7 = 2,800 matches per cell (±0.9pp). Source: tools/sweep_interactive_levers.gd.

The bug, found: the interactive AI used none of your tools — no Boost, no DRS, no adaptive hunt/milk. It played one fixed line, so aggression was uncontested and free. The fix: the AI now plays a difficulty-scaled brain. The "go aggro" bonus collapsed from +19 win-points to +1.5.

Aggression's payoff vs opponent intelligence

Each bar = how many win-points you gain by playing all levers aggressively instead of doing nothing. The smarter the AI, the smaller the free lunch.

What ships: the entry tour, floored to a competent opponent

The difficulty ladder dumbs the entry tour to a 60% random opponent (a league-realism device). In a 1-v-1 a random opponent plays badly and hands you wins — so we floor the interactive opponent at competent textbook. Difficulty still scales above that as you climb (static_eq → adaptive).

A competent opponent punishes reckless aggression (going aggro drops you to 40.7%). The value moved to Boost/DRS (small), and Key Moments became a real tactical choice — with no Key-Moment redesign. It fell out of giving the AI a brain.
