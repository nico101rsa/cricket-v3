Interactive match — is it balanced?

Per-match win-rate for the weakest team in the league (Club slot-0, 1.5★, reference 35/30/30/30 build) vs the seven club opponents (2.0–4.5★), default tour "Flat & Warm". Measured through the real MatchSession controller. N = 600 seasons × 7 fixtures = 4,200 matches per arm (±0.7pp). Source: tools/sweep_interactive_levers.gd.

Verdict: balanced. The worst team wins 30.8% with no levers and tops out at a 50.4% ceiling with optimal lever play — a coin-flip for an underdog. Healthy. No nerf needed.

The one open question (a feel call, not a balance bug)

Key Moments have a strictly-best answer (aggressive), because the choice overrides your batting intent for the whole rest of the phase. That's correct cricket against weak bowling — but it means the "decision" has a right answer. Two ways to read it:

A. Keep it as a learnable phase-strategy chooser (the answer flips on harder tours). B. Make it a state-dependent dilemma (aggression risks a collapse when wickets are scarce) — best designed alongside the upcoming bowling Key Moments so both are genuine trade-offs. Your call.
