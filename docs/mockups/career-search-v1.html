Career search v1 — the optimal line (E4)

9 career lines — how you climb × how you spend

Each cell = one strategy run as full careers from a fresh 1.5★ Club underdog (card 11/11/11/11) to beating Province Premier. Big number = % of careers that finished (count underneath). Blue = time-to-beat: median hours of match play to finish (median seasons in grey). The gold-bordered cell is the optimal line — fastest among the lines that finish reliably.

What the three climb lines mean

Rush — go straight up: cross to the next League the moment you beat its gate (Tour 4 "Day Mixed"), without bothering to win the current League's title. Fewest seasons spent, but you face each new League under-built.
Farm — grind the richest tours (Premier + its super-prize) at your current League until your card is fully maxed (all four attributes at 60), then cross up. Buys full strength before facing harder cricket.
Trophy — win each League's Premier final before moving up (this is exactly the old "naive" line the earlier career-loop eyeball used, so its three cells double as a regression check). The completionist path: richest, strongest, slowest.
The spend columns are the existing Kit Room presets: attr_only (always train the lowest attribute), joker_only (always buy the best joker you can, never train), balanced (buy a scarce Rare/Legendary when one's affordable, else train).
